01 — About

We walk where the roads run out. Small groups, local guides and mountain huts with a view worth every single step.

Founded by mountain guides and lifelong hikers, we organise guided hiking holidays on five continents. We take care of the routes, permits, huts and luggage — you bring your boots and your curiosity.

03 — Advantages

Every step taken care of

  • 01

    Certified mountain guides

    Every group is led by a qualified local guide who knows the trail, the weather and the best place for lunch.

  • 02

    Small groups, twelve at most

    Enough company to share the view, few enough that the trail still feels like your own.

  • 03

    Your luggage goes ahead

    Carry only a daypack. Your bags are waiting for you at the next hut or hotel.

  • 04

    Weather-smart planning

    We check the forecast every day and adjust the route, so you walk the high passes on the clearest days.
